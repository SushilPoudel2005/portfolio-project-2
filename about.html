About Me

Data science student, researcher, tutor, and someone who enjoys making numbers easy to understand.

Who I am

Hello! I'm Sushil Poudel

I'm a B.A. Applied Mathematics & Data Science student at the University of Idaho (expected graduation May 2028) with a 3.66 GPA and a place on the Dean's List. My coursework spans statistics, data analysis, machine learning, deep learning and applied AI engineering.

I enjoy the whole data lifecycle: collecting and cleaning records, building models, and presenting results in a way that helps people decide. As Lead TA and Data Analyst at the Soil Stewards Student Farm, I helped cut unexpected seasonal expenses by 15% using crop-plantation curves and cost forecasts.

Outside of analysis, I care a lot about teaching. As a tutor at the Math Assistance Center I worked with more than 500 students, and that experience shapes how I write documentation and design dashboards for non-technical audiences.
