Privacy Policy

What information this site collects, why, and how it's protected.

On this page

1. Information I collect 2. How your data is used 3. Third-party services 4. Data security 5. Your rights

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1Information I collect

I only collect the personal information you choose to give me through the contact, sign-in or sign-up forms. This may include:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your organization or role (optional)
  • The content of any message you send

2How your data is used

Your information is used only to reply to your messages, manage your account, and occasionally let you know about new projects if you have signed up. I never sell, rent or trade your personal details to advertisers or any third party.

3Third-party services

The Site loads resources from trusted providers: Bootstrap and Bootstrap Icons via jsDelivr, fonts from Google Fonts, and an embedded map from Google Maps on the contact page. These providers may receive basic technical data such as your IP address. Please see their privacy policies for details.

4Data security

I take sensible precautions to protect the information you share from loss or misuse. Still, no transmission over the internet is 100% secure, so please avoid sending sensitive information such as passwords or financial details by message.

5Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information I hold about you at any time. Just send a request through the contact page or email intern.sushil@gmail.com.
