Cookie Policy

A simple explanation of cookies and how this site uses them.

On this page

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. They help sites remember things such as whether you are signed in or which preferences you chose. Similar technologies include local storage and tracking pixels.

2How this site uses cookies

This portfolio does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Any cookies set while you visit come from essential features or embedded third-party content, as listed below.

3Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings. Most browsers let you refuse all cookies, accept only some, or clear them when you close the window. If you block essential cookies, some features such as staying signed in may not work.

4Updates to this policy

If the Site starts using new cookies, this page will be updated. If you have questions, please get in touch.

Frequently asked questions

Does this site track me across other websites?

No. There are no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies on this portfolio.

Will the site still work if I block cookies?

Yes. Browsing works normally, though the embedded map or "Remember me" may not.
