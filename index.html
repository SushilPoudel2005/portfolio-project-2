What I do

Turning data into decisions

I study Applied Mathematics & Data Science at the University of Idaho. I like work where careful analysis has a real effect, whether that's on a student farm, a forestry research lab, or a classroom.

Data Analysis & SQL

I clean, reshape and de-duplicate tables with Python and SQL so the numbers behind a decision can be trusted. Then I turn them into summaries people can actually read.

Machine Learning

From feature engineering to Random Forest and XGBoost models, I build complete pipelines and explore structure in data with K-Means, PCA and t-SNE.

Clear Communication

After tutoring 500+ students, I'm good at explaining technical ideas in plain language, whether through a Streamlit dashboard or a well-documented tool.

A little about me

Curious by nature, analytical by training

I started university in August 2024 and quickly found that I enjoy the moment a messy spreadsheet becomes a clear story. Today I lead data analysis for the Soil Stewards Student Farm, where I track sales, attendance and crop costs.

I also work as an undergraduate researcher in the College of Natural Resources. There I'm building a Python pipeline that estimates tree stem diameter and taper from LiDAR point clouds, and documenting it so future students can extend it.
